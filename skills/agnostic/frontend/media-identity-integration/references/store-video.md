# Store video

This is the optional final production phase of Brand Captures. Start only for an explicit video request. Reuse the shared [state boundary](brand-captures.md#preserve-the-project-and-isolate-runtime-changes), [locale reconnaissance and selection](brand-captures.md#discover-and-select-locales), [production brief](brand-captures.md#prepare-a-production-brief), and [asset-save rules](brand-captures.md#save-only-the-authorized-assets).

## Assemble the approved handoff

Gather the approved captures, store compositions, visual-direction record, copy, logo, palette, typography, and any authorized footage available from the earlier phases. Keep source IDs, locales, targets, release version, and approval status traceable. Reuse prior work without requiring the earlier phases to run again or their assets to be committed.

Preserve the approved visual direction. If artwork or another input does not exist, identify whether it is needed for this video and resolve the gap; do not silently treat unreviewed material as approved or require unrelated static exports.

Extend the production brief with the intended message, supported actions to demonstrate, selected locales, requested stores and target profiles, duration and aspect constraints, audio expectations, and required video or companion deliverables. Match captured UI, promotional copy, and any narration or subtitles to each selected locale. Verify current official requirements for those destinations before selecting a rendering or delivery profile.

Completion: cinetic has a concrete brief, accessible inputs, the approved identity, and the actual output constraints.

## Execute cinetic

Use [cinetic in video execution mode](cinetic-usage.md#video-execution-mode): read and carry out its actual workflow rather than copying its instructions or replacing it with a locally invented video process. Keep cinetic itself unchanged.

Create its film project outside the repository. Supply the prior assets, message, and direction through its supported brief and brand inputs. Use its direction, scene, motion, audio where applicable, rendering, and review capabilities to produce a complete reviewable draft.

At its music and effects selection steps, follow the [sound audition gallery](#audition-sound-candidates) below before finalizing unresolved audio choices.

Demonstrate supported product behavior truthfully. Approved screenshots can be composited and animated as images; they are not evidence that a fabricated interaction occurred. Where the destination or story requires real interaction footage, acquire or request that evidence using the capture phase's isolation and target boundaries. Do not infer additional features from an attractive animation.

Keep original assets intact and maintain links from video scenes to their source material. Store-specific constraints and the supplied identity govern adaptations of cinetic's defaults. If its supported workflow cannot produce the required result within the agreed scope, report that concrete limitation instead of claiming a compliant render.

Completion: a whole playable draft exists and the applicable cinetic checks have real, reported results.

## Audition sound candidates

Offer a playable gallery for unresolved music and effect choices during video creation, and for the affected sounds during revisions. Reuse approved choices and honor an explicit request to choose on the user's behalf. Skip this step for a silent deliverable.

Use cinetic's audio sourcing, licensing, and listening workflow to identify candidates relevant to each role, such as confirmation, typing, or transition. Make all suitable candidates from the consulted sources available to browse, without a fixed per-role quota. Highlight recommended candidates while keeping the others accessible. Include music only when its choice is unresolved. Keep candidates, previews, and selection records in the external film workspace.

Show the real audio in an interactive gallery through the host's rendering protocol. Group candidates by role, with localized labels, stable IDs, duration, source and license links, and play/replay controls. Use filtering or pagination to keep large collections browsable. Use comparable audition levels, user-initiated playback, and one active player at a time. Preview the actual take or excerpt under consideration; record its source, trim, and processing so the selected sound can be reproduced.

For repeated effects such as typing, include a short representative sequence. When footage is available, let the user compare their selected candidates on the same short scene with the current music, identifying isolated versus mixed previews. Keep the picture and mix settings consistent between alternatives. Follow cinetic for analysis and synchronization; distinguish measured findings from the user's taste.

Render the gallery before reporting it shown. If interactive audio is unavailable, state the limitation and offer individually playable clips. A filename list or silent mockup does not provide an audition. Accept choices by stable ID, requests for more alternatives, or omission of an optional effect. Use explicit chat instructions or selection feedback exposed by the host; playback and unreported clicks are not approval.

Carry settled choices into cinetic's supported audio inputs, preserving them through later automatic selection or refinement. Reopen only affected choices when feedback or a concrete technical issue requires it. Selection settles the sound candidate; the resulting mix remains part of video review.

Completion: the relevant choices are settled by explicit selection, retained approval, or delegated choice, and the selected sources are traceable. If the user is reviewing candidates, continue independent work while leaving those choices pending.

## Review and produce target exports

Show playable drafts using the host's media support, organized by locale and target with labels for the destination, version, and output profile. Include poster thumbnails or a contact sheet when helpful, but do not substitute them for playback of the actual video. Report an unavailable playback capability rather than claiming the video was shown.

Iterate the video in response to user feedback. Prior approval of static artwork fixes the supplied direction; the video's story, sequence, rhythm, and sound still need their own review. Preserve the direction unless the user changes it, and propagate shared corrections consistently.

Once the video direction is accepted, render the requested locale and target variants and show the final playable exports together for review and selection, including localized text, narration, and subtitles when present. Adapt each variant to its destination requirements rather than assuming one master file is accepted everywhere. Mark unavailable combinations and incomplete variants explicitly.

Completion: the user has reviewed the actual localized final outputs and explicitly approved the selection for the covered locales and targets. One approval can cover the entire displayed selection; reuse valid approval for unchanged assets. Approval of the direction does not approve unseen localized exports.

## Verify and deliver

Run cinetic's applicable finishing and quality checks, then verify the real exports against the recorded destination requirements: container and codec, dimensions and aspect, duration and frame rate, audio properties when present, and required companion files. Check playback, framing, readable content, and the final approved direction; report the checks actually performed.

Retain the licensing and provenance records required by cinetic for fonts, footage, and sound in the external production workspace and keep them accessible with delivery. Do not infer permission to add source media, rendering code, dependencies, or records to the repository.

Prepare an exact file map using the final video layout in the shared save rules, with agreed variant subdirectories and companion exports if requested. Apply the shared save rules: selection approval and repository-write authorization are separate, and valid prior authorization is reused.

Report which videos were rendered, reviewed, approved, saved, and verified for their destinations. Store upload or publication is a separate explicit action; producing the video does not authorize it.
